### SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM & THE PARADIGM SHIFT (0:00 – 0:30)

**Visual:**

**Narration (Speaker):**  
*"Every month, Emirates Group HR processes thousands of critical transactions—promotions, flight crew reinstatements, housing allowances, and exits. Today, compliance relies on manual sampling—reviewing barely 5 to 10% of files by hand. Critical breaches surface months late, audit coverage is uneven, and audit prep demands frantic manual effort.*

*Welcome to the Emirates Continuous HR Compliance Intelligence Platform. We replace sampling with 100% continuous pre-payroll and post-transaction assurance, explaining every exception in plain language, routing it for human action, and protecting employee fairness."*

### SCENE 4: NO-CODE NLP RULE STUDIO (1:45 – 2:15)

**Visual:**  
Speaker clicks the **No-Code NLP Rule Studio** tab. 
In the prompt box, the speaker types:
`Flag any housing allowance exceeding 12000 AED for Grade 6 or below`
Clicks **Parse & Simulate Rule**. 
Within 300ms, the screen displays:
- Extracted Target Type: `Housing Allowance`
- Amount Threshold: `> AED 12,000`
- Grade Constraint: `<= Grade 6`
- Simulated Impact: `17 exceptions matched (0.17% of batch)`
- Live table of matched transactions.
- Speaker clicks **Deploy Rule to Active Registry**, showing the rule activated in the engine.

**Narration (Speaker):**  
*"Policies evolve rapidly. What happens when Group HR introduces a new allowance cap?*

*With our No-Code NLP Rule Studio, an analyst simply types the policy in plain English: 'Flag any housing allowance exceeding 12,000 AED for Grade 6 or below.'*

*Our NLP compiler extracts the entities, operators, and thresholds into an executable AST filter. In milliseconds, it simulates the impact across all 10,000 transactions, previews the 17 affected staff, and allows the analyst to deploy the rule immediately without waiting months for IT development."*
